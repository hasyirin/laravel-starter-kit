# Md

## Write for people in the spirit of ASD-STE100
Write all text that people read (docs, architecture notes, ADRs, rules, and READMEs) in the spirit of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. Keep sentences short: 20 words at most for an instruction, 25 for a description. Give one idea per sentence and one topic per paragraph. Use the active voice and simple tenses. Write steps as commands, and put a condition first: "If the test fails, check the log." Use common words, and use one term for one thing. Keep code names, commands, paths, and messages exact. Simplify the words, not the facts.
